01 — The Problem

Why is Non-Contrast CT Hard?

On contrast-enhanced CT (CTPA), iodine dye makes blood vessels glow bright white.
On non-contrast CT, the intensity cue disappears completely.

Contrast-Enhanced CT (Easy)

Surrounding Tissue
~40 HU
Blood Vessel (with Dye)
200–400 HU

Massive gap → AI easily separates vessels from tissue. Baseline model achieves Dice = 0.8756.

Non-Contrast CT (Hard)

Surrounding Tissue
~40 HU
Blood Vessel (no Dye)
~45 HU

Near-zero gap → Vessels are invisible to intensity-based models. The AI must learn 3D anatomical shape, not brightness.

Clinical Motivation: Automated 3D MPA:Aorta diameter ratio from non-contrast CT enables non-invasive pulmonary hypertension screening on routine chest CTs — flagging 90% of PH cases vs. 71% with manual 2D measurements.

02 — Our Approach

Four Technical Pillars

We bridge the contrast → non-contrast domain gap with a multi-pronged strategy.

🧪

A. Contrast Dampening

Custom RandContrastDampend augmentation synthetically strips contrast from labelled CTPA scans by blending vessel voxels toward soft-tissue HU (~50), forcing the CNN to learn shape, not brightness.

40% probability Target: 50 HU
🎯

B. Boundary-Aware Loss

Combined DiceCE Loss (region overlap) + Hausdorff DT Loss (boundary distance) maintains crisp vessel contours on tubular anatomy.

ℒ = ℒDiceCE + 0.3 × ℒHausdorff
📚

C. Two-Phase Curriculum

Phase 1: Joint training on contrast + non-contrast data for domain-bridging generalization.
Phase 2: Exclusive non-contrast fine-tuning at 10× lower LR for target specialization.

Transfer Learning Early Stopping
🔬

D. Strict NC Validation

Dedicated held-out non-contrast validation split (6 cases) evaluated every 5 epochs with per-class Dice (Pulmonary Artery + Aorta separately). Zero data leakage between train and val.

Per-Class Dice 22% Hold-out

04 — Phase 1: Joint Domain Bridging

Phase 1: Joint Training

Bridge the domain gap by training simultaneously on contrast and non-contrast data with synthetic contrast removal.

Training Data

  • 26 Contrast CT scans (with labels)
  • 22 Non-Contrast CT scans (train split)
  • Total: 48 training cases

Key Augmentation

RandContrastDampend is applied only to contrast scans with 40% probability, blending vessel voxels from ~300 HU → ~50 HU. The model sees the same anatomy with and without contrast across epochs.

Hyperparameters

  • Learning Rate: 1×10⁻⁴
  • Scheduler: Cosine Annealing
  • Epochs: 100
  • Best NC Dice: 0.8036 (epoch 75)

05 — Phase 2: Target Specialization

Phase 2: NC Fine-Tuning

Drop contrast data. Specialize exclusively on non-contrast anatomy at a 10× lower learning rate.

What Changes

  • All contrast scans removed from training
  • Only 22 NC training cases remain
  • Initialized from Phase 1 best checkpoint
  • No RandContrastDampend (all data is already NC)

Why 10× Lower LR?

A large learning rate would destroy the 3D vascular features learned in Phase 1 (catastrophic forgetting). The tiny LR = 1×10⁻⁵ gently nudges convolution filters to adapt to soft-tissue boundaries without destabilizing the network.

Early Stopping

With only 22 cases, overfitting is a risk. If validation Dice doesn't improve for 20 consecutive epochs, training halts automatically. Best weights are always retained.

08 — Clinical Impact & Future Work

Clinical Impact

🫀

Downstream Application

Our segmentation enables automated 3D MPA:Aorta diameter ratio measurement from routine non-contrast chest CTs. This ratio is a validated biomarker for Pulmonary Hypertension (PH) that correctly flags 90% of PH cases — compared to 71% with manual 2D measurements (Rossdale et al., Thorax 2025).

🌍

Population-Scale Screening

Non-contrast CT is performed millions of times annually for lung cancer screening, trauma, and routine chest imaging. By enabling automated PH screening on these existing scans, we eliminate the need for additional contrast-enhanced imaging — reducing cost, radiation, and contrast-related adverse events.

🔮

Future Work

  • CycleGAN Translation: Synthetic non-contrast ↔ contrast for additional training pairs
  • Shape Priors: Anatomical atlas constraints for post-hoc regularization
  • clDice Loss: Centerline-aware Dice for thin vessel branch accuracy
  • Scale to CT-RATE: Leverage 21,000+ unlabelled NC CTs via pseudo-labels
🏥

Integration Path

  • Export as ONNX/TorchScript for clinical deployment
  • Feed segmentation into IngeniumAI's existing MPA measurement pipeline
  • PACS integration for automated flagging of at-risk patients
  • Validation on external multi-site cohorts
